What is Raghim?

An AI platform that answers from your own documents and data

In one minute

Raghim lets you upload your organization's documents and data, then ask questions in plain language and get answers based on what you uploaded, with the sources shown. You can also build agent flows, which are automated assistants that search documents, query databases, send messages and hand off to people, and put a chat assistant on your website or WhatsApp.

It runs as a hosted service at app.raghim.com or on your own servers, including fully offline.

What you can do with it

Ask your documents

Upload files and ask questions in English or French. Answers come from your documents, not from the open internet.

Automate with flows

Chain document search, database queries and messaging into an assistant that runs on its own.

Embed a chat widget

Put an assistant on your website that answers customers from your knowledge base.

Connect your tools

Use Raghim from code through the API, or from Claude, Cursor and Codex through MCP.

Key terms

  • Collection: a named group of documents. You choose which collections the assistant may use for a question.
  • Agent: the chat assistant. It answers from your collections and can also use database tables.
  • Agent flow: a saved, step-by-step assistant you design on a canvas. It starts at a Start node, runs tools and logic, and ends with a reply.
  • Widget: an embeddable chat window for your website, powered by a collection or a flow.
  • Deploy: making a saved flow live, so the Agent and widgets use it.
  • MCP: an open standard that lets AI tools such as Claude and Cursor connect to outside systems. See MCP Setup.
